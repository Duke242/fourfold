# Changelog

Each release bumps `APP_VERSION` in `index.html` and adds a section here and to the `CHANGES` list in `index.html`, which players see under "What's new" in the game. Newest first.

## 2.0.0 — 2026-11-17
- New look, “Card Room”, the same as our 400 game: a walnut top bar with brass trim, brass buttons, and a deep green felt with a fine grain
- New cards with rich colours, cream edges and a round badge in the middle
- Players sit around the table as name tags, and whoever is playing glows
- Cards are thrown onto the pile on a gentle arc, and land at a slightly random angle and spot
- Cards are dealt and drawn from the deck, so you can see where every card goes
- Slam: tap Slam, or press and hold a card until it shakes and then swipe it up, to throw it down hard. Bots slam sometimes too
- Sound effects: cards on the felt, dealing, a chime when it's your turn, the slam, and applause when you win. The speaker button turns them off
- On phones, tap a card to lift it and tap again to play it, or swipe it up
- End of a round: a banner, with confetti when you win it
- Winning the game brings a trophy, confetti and the final scores counting up
- A Scores button shows every round of the game
- Your game against the bots is kept when you reload the page
- Version number and a "What's new" list

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-20
- Plays by the official rules, with Wild Draw Four challenges and “Last card!” catches
- Built for phones first, in portrait or landscape
- Play online with up to four friends using a 4-character room code
